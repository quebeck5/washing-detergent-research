SECTION 2: HAZARDS IDENTIFICATION

Signal Word: DANGER
• H318: Causes serious eye damage (Category 1).
• H315: Causes skin irritation (Category 2).
• H334: May cause allergy or asthma symptoms if inhaled (Category 1).
• H335: May cause respiratory irritation (Category 3).

Precautionary Statements:

  • P102: Keep out of reach of children.
  • P260: Do not breathe dust.
  • P280: Wear eye protection and gloves for prolonged contact.
  • P305+P351+P338: IF IN EYES: Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses if present and easy to do. Continue rinsing.
  • P310 / P313: Immediately call Poisons Information Centre (13 11 26) or seek medical advice.
  • P302+P352: IF ON SKIN: Wash with plenty of soap and water.
  • P301+P330+P331: IF SWALLOWED: Rinse mouth. Do NOT induce vomiting.

SECTION 4: FIRST AID MEASURES

  • Eye: Flush eyes with running water for at least 15 minutes holding eyelids open. Seek medical attention immediately.
  • Skin: Wash contaminated skin with water. Remove contaminated clothing.
  • Inhalation: Remove to fresh air. If irritation or respiratory symptoms persist, seek medical advice.
  • Ingestion: Rinse mouth. Drink 1–2 glasses of water slowly. Do NOT induce vomiting. Call 13 11 26 immediately.

SECTION 5: FIRE FIGHTING MEASURES

Non-flammable granular solid. In case of surrounding fire, use water spray, dry chemical, carbon dioxide, or foam. Wear SCBA.

SECTION 6: ACCIDENTAL RELEASE MEASURES

Prevent airborne dust. Sweep up mechanically using dry broom or vacuum with HEPA filter into clean labeled container. Prevent large quantities from entering stormwater drains.

SECTION 7: HANDLING & STORAGE

Store in a cool, dry place below 35 °C. Protect from moisture. Keep container tightly closed and out of reach of children. Do not mix with chlorine bleach.

SECTION 8: EXPOSURE CONTROLS & PERSONAL PROTECTION

Safe Work Australia TWA for nuisance dust = 10 mg/m³. Provide adequate natural ventilation. Wear safety glasses and protective gloves.

SECTION 9: PHYSICAL & CHEMICAL PROPERTIES

SECTION 10: STABILITY & REACTIVITY

Stable under recommended storage conditions. Incompatible with strong acids and sodium hypochlorite (chlorine bleach).

SECTION 11: TOXICOLOGICAL INFORMATION

Oral LD50 (rat) > 2000 mg/kg. Causes serious eye damage/irritation. Skin irritation Category 2. Inhalation of enzyme dust may cause sensitization.

SECTION 12: ECOLOGICAL INFORMATION

Surfactants are readily biodegradable per AS 4351. Contains zero added phosphorus.

SECTION 13: DISPOSAL CONSIDERATIONS

Dispose of packaging in municipal recycling stream. Unused product should be neutralized and disposed per local EPA guidelines.
