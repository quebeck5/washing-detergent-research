## 🔬 Research Overview

### 1. Cold Power Laundry Liquids (Henkel Australia)
* **Products:** *Cold Power Advanced Clean* vs. *Cold Power Extreme Clean*
* **SDS Source:** Henkel SAP Gateway Portal (SDS Nos. 565969 & 729522, Issued January 2026)
* **Core Differences Researched:**
  * **Enzyme Stabilization Matrix:** Extreme Clean replaces Dipropylene Glycol with a synergistic **Sorbitol + Glycerin** polyol system. In the concentrated liquid, this forms a reversible cyclic borate ester chelate with Sodium Metaborate that binds to the Serine-221 catalytic site of subtilisin (protease), preventing autodigestion of companion enzymes (amylase, cellulase, mannanase) during storage. Upon 1:1000 wash dilution, the complex dissociates instantly, freeing 100% catalytic activity.
  * **Fragrance Fixation & Fabric Substantivity:** Extreme Clean adds advanced synthetic fixatives (**Iso E Super, Hedione, and macrocyclic musk**) with low vapor pressure and high hydrophobicity that adsorb to fabrics through the rinse cycle, providing long-lasting fragrance on dry clothes.

### 2. Biozet Attack Laundry Powders (Kao Corporation / Australia)
* **Products:** *Regular Powder*, *PLUS Softener Powder*, *PLUS Eliminator Powder*
* **SDS & Ingredient Source:** Kao Corporation Australia ingredient disclosure & GHS 7 specifications
* **Core Differences Researched:**
  * **Bleach & Sanitizing System:**
    * *Regular:* Sodium Percarbonate (standard oxygen bleach releasing $H_2O_2$).
    * *PLUS Eliminator:* Sodium Percarbonate + **DOBS** (Sodium Dodecanoyloxybenzene Sulfonate) bleach activator. Generates lipophilic **perlauric acid** in situ at 20 °C, penetrating bacterial cell membranes for 99.9% bacterial kill (*Moraxella osloensis*) and sweat malodour destruction.
    * *PLUS Softener:* 0% Bleach (Bleach-free to preserve soft textures and delicate dyes).
  * **Fabric Softening:** PLUS Softener uses natural mineral **Bentonite Clay** platelets (~1 nm thick) that mechanically lubricate cotton fibers, providing fluffiness while keeping bath towels **100% hydrophilic and water-absorbent** (unlike traditional liquid quaternary softeners that coat fibers in water-repellent wax).
  * **Enzymes & Optical Brighteners:** Omitted in *Eliminator* to prevent oxidative denaturation by perlauric acid and to avoid metameric color shifting on dark and technical synthetic athletic wear.

## 📊 Market & Commercial Strategy

Why do manufacturers produce distinct formulations sold at the exact same retail price point?
1. **FMCG "Shelf Blocking":** Multiplying SKUs captures 6–12 consecutive supermarket facings at Coles and Woolworths, blocking competitor brands from consumer view.
2. **Target Personas:**
   * *The Benchmark Purist:* Maximum lab-test cleaning scores (Regular / Advanced).
   * *The Towel Care Pragmatist:* Built-in natural softness with zero waxy towel buildup (Softener).
   * *The Activewear Athlete:* Anti-permastink biofilm eradication on synthetics without fabric fading (Eliminator).
   * *The Sensory Seeker:* Long-lasting fragrance on dry linen as proof of cleanliness (Extreme Clean).
3. **Compensatory Cost Engineering:** Premium additions (DOBS activator, macrocyclic musks) are balanced by omitting enzymes/brighteners or using high-volume agricultural polyols (glycerin/sorbitol).
