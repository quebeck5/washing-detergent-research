Executive Summary: The Formulation Ladder from $1.25/kg to $13.00/kg

Across Coles supermarkets in Victoria, laundry powder pricing follows a strict chemical hierarchy. While budget shoppers see Coles Simply (4kg for $5.00) as a bargain, a laboratory breakdown reveals why the benchmark Biozet Attack Regular commands a 5x to 10x higher price:

1. The "Inert Bulking Salt" Ratio: In Coles Simply Powder ($1.25/kg), approximately 70% of the box is inert Sodium Sulfate filler. In contrast, Biozet Attack Regular cuts filler to ~25%, replacing it with insoluble Zeolite A water softeners and a concentrated triple-surfactant system (Sodium Alkyl Sulfate + Alcohol Ethoxylate + Soap).

2. Biocatalytic Cleavage (Enzymes): Coles Simply contains 0% enzymes (it cannot digest set-in food or protein stains). Fab introduces a single Protease enzyme. Coles Ultra steps up to dual enzymes (Protease + Amylase). Biozet Attack Regular provides the complete biocatalytic trifecta: Protease + Amylase + Care Cellulase (which shaves damaged microfibrils, eliminates fabric fuzz, and stops color graying).

3. Oxygen Bleaching: Coles Simply and Fab contain 0% bleaching agents (they cannot chemically oxidize coffee, tea, or red wine chromophores). Biozet Attack Regular packs 10–15% Sodium Percarbonate, releasing reactive oxygen in the wash to actively whiten and sanitize fabrics.

3. Deep Chemical Research: The Mechanics of Powder Detergency

3.1 The Inert Bulking Trap: Why 4kg for $5.00 Delivers Less Cleaning Power

The single biggest operational difference between ultra-budget powder and premium powder is the Sodium Sulfate ($ ext{Na}_2 ext{SO}_4$) content:

Coles Simply ($1.25/kg) vs. Biozet Attack Regular:

• In Coles Simply, Sodium Sulfate makes up 65% to 72% of total carton weight. In a standard 100g scoop, you are throwing 70g of non-cleaning salt into your machine and only ~5g of active surfactant.

• In Biozet Attack Regular, Sodium Sulfate is reduced to ~25%. The remaining 75% consists of active functional chemistry: ~20% Zeolite A water softeners, ~22% active surfactants, and ~12% oxygen bleach.

The Math: A single compact 30g scoop of Biozet Attack Regular delivers more than double the active surfactant molecules and infinite times more bleach/enzymes than a massive 100g scoop of Coles Simply!

Why Cheap Powders Ruin Garden Soil & Septic Tanks:

Because Coles Simply Powder contains massive quantities of sodium sulfate and sodium carbonate, the wash runoff has an exceptionally high Sodium Adsorption Ratio (SAR).

Soil Breakdown: Sodium ions displace essential calcium and magnesium ions on clay soil particles, causing clay deflocculation (soil collapse). The soil becomes impermeable to water and suffocates plant roots. Coles Simply Powder should never be diverted to garden greywater.

3.2 Biocatalytic Cleavage: Zero Enzymes vs. Protease vs. Biozet's Multi-Enzyme Engine

Enzymes are expensive biological catalysts produced via industrial fermentation. They represent the clear line separating basic dirt detergents from true stain-removing powerhouses:

Tier 1: Zero Enzymes (Coles Simply)

Contains 0% enzymes. Can only wash away surface dust and loose sweat. Completely unable to break down biological proteins (blood, egg, grass, body sebum) or starches (gravy, pasta, rice) in cold water without aggressive pre-soaking and manual scrubbing.

Tier 2: Single Protease (Fab Sunshine Fresh)

Henkel includes a single Subtilisin Protease enzyme in Fab. This provides basic digestion of protein soils, but Fab lacks amylase (leaving starch soils behind) and lacks oxygen bleach.

Tier 3: Multi-Enzyme Cocktail (Biozet Attack)

Biozet incorporates Protease (cleaves proteins), Alpha-Amylase (liquefies sticky starch glues), and Care Cellulase. Cellulase micro-shaves frayed cotton microfibrils, releasing entrapped soot particles and preventing the dull "gray veil" on cotton clothes.

3.3 Oxygen Bleach & Zeolite Water Softeners (Why Whites Stay White)

Sodium Percarbonate (Active Oxygen Bleach):

• Coles Simply and Fab contain 0% bleach. Over time, white clothes washed in bleach-free powders develop a yellow or grayish dinginess because oxidizable tannins and colored body soils never get bleached away.

• Biozet Attack Regular contains 10–15% Sodium Percarbonate. When dissolved, it releases hydrogen peroxide ($H_2O_2$) that oxidizes colored stains into colorless water-soluble fragments.

Zeolite A vs. Soda Ash Alone:

• Cheap powders rely purely on Sodium Carbonate to soften water. While carbonate precipitates calcium as chalky calcium carbonate ($ ext{CaCO}_3$), this precipitate deposits onto fabric fibers and machine heating elements as stiff scale.

• Coles Ultra and Biozet Regular incorporate Zeolite A (Sodium Aluminosilicate). Zeolites are rigid 3D microporous mineral cages that trap $ ext{Ca}^{2+}$ and $ ext{Mg}^{2+}$ ions via non-precipitating ion exchange, leaving fabrics soft and free of chalky residue.

5. Victorian Water Quality & Dosing Guidance for Laundry Powders

  • Melbourne Soft Water Benefit: In Greater Melbourne (Yarra Ranges catchment, 10–25 mg/L CaCO₃), soft water helps cheap powders like Coles Simply and Fab perform better than they would interstate. Because there is virtually no calcium hardness, you can dose ~20% less powder to avoid chalky fabric stiffness.
  • Regional Victoria (Geelong, Ballarat, Bendigo): Higher mineral hardness (60–150 mg/L) causes Coles Simply and Fab to fail because they lack zeolite builders. Calcium precipitates the low surfactant load into sticky soap scum. In regional Victoria, always choose a zeolite-built powder like Coles Ultra or Biozet Attack Regular.
  • Temperature Recommendation: Dissolution kinetics for compact powders (Biozet) are optimized at 20 °C. For high-filler powders (Coles Simply), ensure your dispenser drawer is wiped completely dry before dosing to prevent gumming and clumping.
