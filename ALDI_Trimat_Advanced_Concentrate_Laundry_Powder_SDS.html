SECTION 4: FIRST AID MEASURES

  • Eye: Flush eyes with running water for at least 15 minutes holding eyelids apart. Seek immediate medical attention.
  • Skin: Wash contaminated skin with water. Remove contaminated clothing.
  • Inhalation: Remove to fresh air. If irritation or asthma symptoms persist, seek medical advice immediately.
  • Ingestion: Rinse mouth. Drink 1–2 glasses of water. Do NOT induce vomiting. Call Poisons Info 13 11 26.

SECTION 5: FIRE FIGHTING MEASURES

Non-flammable granular solid. In case of surrounding fire, use water spray, dry chemical, carbon dioxide, or foam. High heat may liberate oxygen gas from percarbonate.

SECTION 6: ACCIDENTAL RELEASE MEASURES

Prevent airborne dust. Sweep up mechanically using dry broom or vacuum with HEPA filter into clean labeled container. Prevent large quantities from entering waterways.

SECTION 7: HANDLING & STORAGE

Store in a cool, dry place below 35 °C. Protect from moisture. Keep container tightly closed and out of reach of children. Do not mix with chlorine bleach.

SECTION 8: EXPOSURE CONTROLS & PERSONAL PROTECTION

Safe Work Australia TWA for nuisance dust = 10 mg/m³; Protease enzyme = 0.00006 mg/m³ peak limitation. Provide adequate ventilation. Wear safety glasses and protective gloves.
