SECTION 4: FIRST AID MEASURES

  • Eye Contact: Immediately hold eyelids open and flush continuously with clean running water for at least 15 minutes. Urgently transport to hospital or seek immediate medical attention.
  • Skin Contact: Immediately remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin thoroughly with abundant water. Wash contaminated clothing before reuse.
  • Inhalation: Remove person to fresh air. If experiencing respiratory symptoms, call a POISON CENTRE (13 11 26) or doctor.
  • Ingestion: Never give anything by mouth to an unconscious person. Do NOT induce vomiting. Rinse mouth thoroughly with water and slowly drink 1-2 glasses of water. Call 13 11 26 immediately.

SECTION 5: FIRE FIGHTING MEASURES

Suitable Extinguishing Media: Water spray jet, carbon dioxide, dry chemical powder, or alcohol-resistant foam. Product itself is non-flammable.
Hazardous Decomposition Products: Combustion or high thermal decomposition may release oxides of carbon, sulfur, sodium, and organic fumes.
Protective Equipment: Wear self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) and full chemical protective clothing.

SECTION 6: ACCIDENTAL RELEASE MEASURES

Avoid generating airborne dust. Prevent spillage from entering municipal stormwater drains or waterways. Collect mechanically with a broom or industrial vacuum with HEPA filter into clean, labeled containers for appropriate disposal. Wash residue with plenty of water.

SECTION 7: HANDLING & STORAGE

Handling: Avoid direct contact with eyes and skin. Avoid breathing dust. Wash hands thoroughly after handling. Keep container tightly closed.
Storage: Store in a cool, dry, well-ventilated location below 40 °C. Protect from moisture and direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children and separate from strong acids and chlorine-containing bleaches.

SECTION 8: EXPOSURE CONTROLS & PERSONAL PROTECTION

National Exposure Standards (Safe Work Australia): Atmospheric nuisance dust: TWA = 10 mg/m³.
Engineering Controls: Ensure adequate ventilation. If dust is generated, use local exhaust ventilation.
Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):
• Eyes: Chemical safety goggles or face shield (AS/NZS 1337).
• Hands: Chemical-resistant protective gloves (nitrile or rubber, AS/NZS 2161).
• Respiratory: Dust respirator (P2 class, AS/NZS 1715/1716) if ventilation is inadequate.

SECTION 10: STABILITY & REACTIVITY

Chemical Stability: Stable under recommended storage conditions.
Incompatible Materials: Strong acids (vigorous exothermic reaction releasing CO₂), chlorine-based bleaching agents (do not mix with chlorine bleach!).
Conditions to Avoid: High humidity, elevated temperatures (> 40 °C), moisture ingress.

SECTION 11: TOXICOLOGICAL INFORMATION

Acute Toxicity: Oral LD50 (rat) calculated > 2000 mg/kg.
Skin Corrosion/Irritation: Irritating to skin (Category 2). Prolonged exposure may cause defatting and dermatitis.
Serious Eye Damage/Irritation: Causes severe and serious eye damage (Category 1). Risk of corneal burns.
Sensitization: Contains NO added enzyme proteins. Not classified as a respiratory sensitizer. Contains low concentration fragrance allergens.

SECTION 12: ECOLOGICAL INFORMATION

Ecotoxicity: Harmful to aquatic life in concentrated form due to alkalinity and surfactants. Diluted wash water is suitable for grey water reuse on gardens.
Surfactant Biodegradability: All surfactants in this formulation comply with Australian standard AS 4351 for ready biodegradability.
Phosphorus: Meets the Australian voluntary environmental standard for zero phosphorus (NP symbol: < 0.5%).

SECTION 13: DISPOSAL CONSIDERATIONS

Dispose of in accordance with local State and Federal EPA waste management regulations. Uncontaminated packaging cardboard/cartons are fully recyclable in municipal paper streams.
