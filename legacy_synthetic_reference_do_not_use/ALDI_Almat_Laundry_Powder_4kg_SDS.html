SECTION 4: FIRST AID MEASURES

  • Eye: Immediately wash eye thoroughly with copious amounts of water for at least 15 minutes while holding eyelids open. Immediately transport to medical care or ophthalmologist.
  • Skin: Wash thoroughly with soap and plenty of water. If irritation develops, seek medical attention.
  • Inhalation: Remove casualty from exposure to fresh air. If asthma or breathing difficulties occur, contact medical doctor immediately.
  • Ingestion: Rinse mouth with water. Drink plenty of water. Never give anything by mouth to an unconscious person. Do not induce vomiting. Call 13 11 26.

SECTION 5: FIRE FIGHTING MEASURES

Product is non-combustible. Use extinguishing media appropriate for surrounding packaging (water spray, dry powder, CO₂). Cool exposed containers with water spray.

SECTION 6: ACCIDENTAL RELEASE MEASURES

Avoid generating dust. Wear dust respirator (P2) and safety glasses. Collect mechanically by dry sweeping or industrial vacuum. Transfer to approved disposal containers.

SECTION 7: HANDLING & STORAGE

Store in a cool, well-ventilated dry area below 30 °C. Protect cartons from humidity and direct sunlight. Keep scoops clean and dry.

SECTION 8: EXPOSURE CONTROLS & PERSONAL PROTECTION

Safe Work Australia TWA for inspirable dust = 10 mg/m³; Protease enzyme = 0.00006 mg/m³ peak limitation. Provide adequate mechanical exhaust in commercial packaging facilities.
