SECTION 4: FIRST AID MEASURES

  • Eye: Immediately flush eyes with gently flowing water for at least 15 minutes holding eyelids apart. Seek medical advice if irritation persists.
  • Skin: Rinse skin with water. Remove contaminated clothing. If irritation develops, seek medical advice.
  • Inhalation: Move subject to fresh air. If coughing or nasal irritation develops, rest and seek medical assistance if symptoms persist.
  • Ingestion: Rinse mouth thoroughly with water. Drink 1–2 glasses of water. Do NOT induce vomiting. Contact Poisons Information Centre (13 11 26).

SECTION 5: FIRE FIGHTING MEASURES

Non-combustible granular bulk powder. In a structural fire, use water spray, carbon dioxide, or dry powder. Hazardous decomposition products: Sulfur oxides (SOx) and Carbon oxides (COx).

SECTION 6: ACCIDENTAL RELEASE MEASURES

Wear dust mask and safety goggles. Sweep up spilled powder mechanically using broom or vacuum into clean containers. Avoid generating dust clouds. Do not wash massive quantities into suburban stormwater drains.

SECTION 7: HANDLING & STORAGE

Store in dry, ambient indoor conditions below 35 °C. Keep carton tightly closed to prevent moisture absorption and caking. Keep out of reach of children.

SECTION 8: EXPOSURE CONTROLS & PERSONAL PROTECTION

Safe Work Australia Exposure Standard: TWA 10 mg/m³ for nuisance dust. Standard household dosing requires no specialized PPE. Wear rubber gloves for manual handwashing.
