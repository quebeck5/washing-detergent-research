SECTION 4: FIRST AID MEASURES

  • Eye: Immediately flush with clean water for at least 15 minutes, holding eyelids open. Seek urgent medical advice.
  • Skin: Wash thoroughly with water. Hypoallergenic formula minimizes dermatitis risk, but concentrated alkaline paste causes irritation.
  • Inhalation: Remove to fresh air. If coughing or throat irritation occurs from dust, seek medical advice.
  • Ingestion: Rinse mouth. Drink 1–2 glasses of water. Do NOT induce vomiting. Call Poisons Information Centre (13 11 26).

SECTION 5: FIRE FIGHTING MEASURES

Non-combustible granular solid. In case of surrounding fire, use water spray, foam, dry powder, or CO₂. Wear full PPE and SCBA.

SECTION 6: ACCIDENTAL RELEASE MEASURES

Avoid generating airborne dust. Sweep up using dry methods or a HEPA-filtered vacuum into labeled containers for disposal. Prevent massive spills from entering municipal waterways.

SECTION 7: HANDLING & STORAGE

Store in a cool, dry area below 35 °C. Protect carton from humidity and moisture. Keep tightly closed and out of reach of children. Do not mix with chlorine bleach.

SECTION 8: EXPOSURE CONTROLS & PERSONAL PROTECTION

Safe Work Australia TWA for nuisance dust = 10 mg/m³. Natural ventilation is sufficient for home use. Wear protective goggles and gloves for bulk handling.
