SECTION 4: FIRST AID MEASURES

  • Eye Contact: Flush with clean running water for at least 15 minutes, holding eyelids open. If discomfort or redness persists, seek medical attention.
  • Skin Contact: Rinse affected skin thoroughly with water. Remove contaminated clothing and wash before reuse.
  • Inhalation: If dust or vapor causes respiratory irritation, move to fresh air. Seek medical advice if breathing difficulties develop.
  • Ingestion: Rinse mouth with water. Drink 1–2 glasses of water. Do not induce vomiting. Call Poisons Information Centre (13 11 26) or a doctor immediately.

SECTION 5: FIRE FIGHTING MEASURES

Suitable Extinguishing Media: Water spray, foam, dry chemical, carbon dioxide. Product is non-flammable.
Hazardous Combustion Products: Oxides of carbon, sulfur, sodium compounds, and organic fumes.
PPE for Firefighters: Standard protective clothing and self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA).

SECTION 6: ACCIDENTAL RELEASE MEASURES

Avoid contact with eyes and skin. Absorb liquid spills with sand or inert absorbent and dispose in accordance with local regulations. Sweep dry powder spills using dry methods, avoiding dust generation. Prevent large untreated quantities from entering stormwater drains.

SECTION 7: HANDLING & STORAGE

Handling: Keep containers tightly closed when not in use. Avoid generating dust or splashing into eyes.
Storage: Store in a cool, dry, well-ventilated area away from direct sunlight, moisture, and extreme temperatures (< 35 °C). Keep out of reach of children and separate from strong acids and chlorine bleach.

SECTION 8: EXPOSURE CONTROLS & PERSONAL PROTECTION

National Exposure Standards: Safe Work Australia TWA for nuisance dust = 10 mg/m³.
Engineering Controls: Natural ventilation is generally adequate for domestic use.
Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Safety glasses/goggles and waterproof gloves recommended during bulk handling or decanting.

SECTION 10: STABILITY & REACTIVITY

Chemical Stability: Stable under ambient storage conditions.
Incompatible Materials: Strong acids (causes vigorous gas evolution/effervescence with carbonates), strong oxidizing agents, sodium hypochlorite (chlorine bleach).
Hazardous Reactions: None known under normal conditions.

SECTION 11: TOXICOLOGICAL INFORMATION

Acute Toxicity: Oral LD50 (rat) calculated > 2000 mg/kg.
Skin Irritation: Category 2 (Causes mild to moderate skin irritation with prolonged contact).
Eye Irritation: Category 1 (Serious Eye Damage due to alkaline builders and sodium percarbonate).
Sensitization: Contains Protease/Subtilisin and Amylase enzymes. Inhalation of dust may produce sensitization.

SECTION 12: ECOLOGICAL INFORMATION

Ecotoxicity: Harmful to aquatic life at high concentrations due to surfactant load. Diluted wash water is suitable for lawn and garden greywater use.
Biodegradability: Surfactants conform to Australian Standard AS 4351 for ultimate biodegradability.
Phosphorus: Meets the Australian NP standard (zero added phosphorus).

SECTION 13: DISPOSAL CONSIDERATIONS

Empty containers are fully recyclable through standard municipal curbside recycling (yellow bin). Rinse container prior to recycling.
